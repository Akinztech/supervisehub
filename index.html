Designed for the whole department

A connected workflow for students, supervisors and coordinators

The platform turns informal supervision into a searchable, auditable and measurable academic process.

01

Session scheduling

Students request meetings, supervisors approve or reschedule them, and outcomes are recorded in a structured log.

02

Milestone tracking

Chapter submissions, reviews and approvals are linked to weighted milestones and a live progress indicator.

03

Actionable feedback

Every session records feedback, decisions, action items and the next agreed follow-up date.

04

Document versions

Project drafts and deliverables are uploaded with descriptions and version control for easier review.

05

Supervisor portfolio

Supervisors monitor assigned students, pending reviews, overdue tasks and upcoming academic meetings.

06

Quality analytics

Coordinators assess supervision frequency, workload, stage progression and at-risk students across the cohort.
